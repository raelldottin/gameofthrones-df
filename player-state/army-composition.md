## Current strategic decisions

- Treat **March 1** as the primary long-term investment march. Moving Venator here creates an S/A/A core and gives it the strongest rank-only profile in the current set.
- Treat **March 4** as the highest-upside specialist march. Kalspire and Sunfyre are elite anchors; Shadowsong is its clear rank-only weak link.
- Keep **March 2** as the balanced core. It has no C-, D-, or F-tier member and should remain practical while the elite Legendary dragons develop.
- Treat **March 3** as a Caraxes-led development march. Moving Venator out lowers the ceiling of the former Vaeldra / Venator / Caraxes trio.
- Keep **March 5** as stable depth. It has no severe public-tier liability, but it lacks an S-tier anchor.
- Among marches 6–10, **March 9** has the best rank-only depth profile. March 10 has the strongest single anchor in Tairax, while marches 6 and 8 contain the two F-tier dragons.
