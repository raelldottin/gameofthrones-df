## Evidence policy
Use the repository's June combat-system and troop-matchup notes, not current November progression to fill June battle stats. Field troop damage modifier +7%/-7%; +20% affinity is a separate dragon-stat bonus and is deliberately not applied as a flat troop damage multiplier. Siege-selected matchup is unknown. Actual game damage formulas, lane targeting, action ordering, status duration/stacking, command procs, gear and enemy stats are not sufficiently established. The aggregate model is explicitly illustrative and not used to alter army plans. No Monte Carlo win odds until a calibrated stochastic model and independent outcome evidence exist.

Normalize explicit starting and ending counts only. Never use capacity as start, never conflate gross kills with net losses. Preserve source path and payload digest. Reject contradictory totals and outcomes. Replay full round records, preserving negative net losses as recovery (not gross healing). Exclude repeated encounter summaries with uncertain carryover, incomplete round records and ambiguous capacities. Exclusions are explicit; no assertion all reports are independent or representative.

## Calibration
A deliberately simple simultaneous aggregate net attrition model uses assumed rates with the documented field troop modifiers. Fit net loss/exposure ratios only from complete traces of the same encounter mode. Hold out entire date groups conservatively, never rows from the same encounter session. Compare normalized terminal troop-count error against no-change baseline; report abstentions, no win probability, no release acceptance. If insufficient same-mode training groups, do not score. These historical selected reports are development evidence, not a prospective holdout. Full mechanical simulation and calibrated prediction remain unimplemented.
