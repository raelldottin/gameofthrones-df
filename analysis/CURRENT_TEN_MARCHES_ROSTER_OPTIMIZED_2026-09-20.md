# September 20, 2026: roster-first ten-march analysis

## Scope and evidence

**Status: proposed, not battle-validated.** Build the plan independently from all 36 owned dragons and their **September 20** star ranks and currently unlocked habit levels in [`player-state/dragon-roster-2026-09-20.json`](../player-state/dragon-roster-2026-09-20.json). Do **not** count locked higher-star abilities as active. Earlier battle reports and the September 3 compositions were **not used to select or order** these teams. Exact current Reign Levels, equipped items, troop quantities, matchup distribution, a validated season-wide PvP meta, and calibrated win rates were not supplied or established; therefore this is a mechanics-guided proposal, not an exact optimization or guaranteed win rate.

## What current-season information changes

The official September **17–23** Kingswood Hunt says White Stag encounters can deal powerful **Physical** damage and Stagger; that suggests examining the Physical suppression and protection of marches **4, 6, 9 or 10**, subject to actual affinity, opponent troop type and current Reign Levels. This event guide cannot establish the PvP meta, a counter to every White Stag, or a universal best march.

The official troop-counter cycle is **Cavalry > Shieldbearers > Archers > Spearmen > Cavalry**. Against a known opponent, compare the appropriate counter-aligned march to this general deployment priority rather than treating row number as overriding troop advantage. Siege troops serve durability damage and are disadvantaged against the four standard combat troops; these ten are combat-focused and not siege-specialist compositions.

The game's official announcement says **up to five** dragon-led armies may be fielded simultaneously. Treat marches **1–5** as an initial deployment shortlist and **6–10** as preset alternatives or rotations; do not claim all ten deploy simultaneously. Reevaluate the shortlist based on current Reign Levels and troop stocks before launching.

## Measurement plan before promoting a team to 'proven'

Record the version and fight mode, all six dragons' star/habit states and Reign Levels, troop counts and counter relation, equipment, winner versus stalemate, damage inflicted, troop survival percentage for **both sides**, round duration, and whether Recovery, Taunt, Stagger, and other conditional effects triggered. Compare repeated similar matchups with one dragon or lane changed at a time. New results must be associated with this roster date; older battle records may remain archived but must not masquerade as measurements of September 20 teams.
