Keep Rhaenyra on march 1 (or move her to march 2 if another commander is not there), and Corlys on march 3. Test Alicent on march 5 (Shadowsong / Arulix / Thunderstrike, Cavalry) for Arulix's Hypnotic Helix Overwhelm/Stagger from Vanguard; this activates Cunning but lacks Willful. Compare against the march 2 Willful build. For a double-trait Archer experiment, test Solstryker / Zivern / Jagadrix under Alicent (Zivern Cunning, Jagadrix Willful, all three Archer-positive according to the prior march and dragon profile); this is an alternative, not simultaneous with Corlys march 3 or original march 2. Zivern's qualifying Control for Beacon is unverified. Do not trade away troop affinity merely to match two traits. Corlys's alternative march 10 (Kalspire / Feskar / Vermax, Cavalry) matches both traits and frees Solstryker and Zivern, but changes deployment and requires avoiding overlaps. Verify current affinity, troop counts and commander seat availability in Army Builder.

## Rhaenyra spending
First unlock Swift Counsel and Heir's Reckoning at rank 1 each: combined 2,000 Medals and zero Honors/Onyx Honors; gain immediate conditional Intelligence and Instinct boosts, Initiative and control chances. Commander rank should reach 5 under official sum-of-talents rule; not observed. Preserve 1,065 Honors initially rather than spending 700 on Realm's Delight 3→4 for a marginal 0.25 percentage-point general damage reduction (0.5 against non-player armies). Realm's Delight retains its rank 3 Strength and tile bonus. If both rank-1 unlocks and Realm upgrade are made at displayed prices, total 3,750 Medals and 700 Honors, leaving 365 Honors; rank 6 projected. No upgrades actually observed or performed in game.

Target rank 1 across all owned commanders' useful talents first where cost allows, then plan ranks 10/20 for milestone bonuses once actual prices and rank 1 effects of Alicent/Corlys are captured. Do not assume their rank-1 costs are identical. At Rhaenyra rank 1 talent chance 2%, control is low reliability; rank 20 chance 30% per round, not guaranteed. Resistance 4 stacks = 20% nominal reduction, Weakened 3 stacks = 15% nominal reduction, each before unknown interaction rules. Avoid claiming exact aggregate tile or damage stacking.

## Validation
Record mode, opponents, current reign levels, troop types, initial and surviving troops, commander/talent ranks, status activations, rounds, tile durability removed, and battle outcomes. Compare same opponent and same formation with and without commander (if practical), repeat trials because chance effects vary. Prioritize troop loss and capture success over displayed power; do not treat proposals as verified wins.
